Our Story

Rooted in Culture,
Crafted with Love

Maya Decoration was born from a deep reverence for Nepali culture. We transform spaces into living tapestries of tradition — where every marigold garland, every draped cloth, every flickering diya tells a story.

From the joyous grandeur of a wedding mandap to the tender intimacy of a rice-feeding ceremony, we bring the same devotion to every event. Our artisans draw from generations of cultural knowledge, weaving authenticity into every detail.
